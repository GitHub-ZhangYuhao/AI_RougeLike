# v08 Soft Cartoon Ground Expansion

This set directly follows the art direction selected from:
- `../../StyleAnchors/v07_soft_cartoon_ground_textures/final/flagstone_cartoon_soft.png`
- `../../StyleAnchors/v07_soft_cartoon_ground_textures/final/stone_moss_soft.png`

## Grass

- `grass_moss_cushion_soft.png`: celadon grass mixed with broad rounded moss cushions; suitable for wet meadows and shrine edges.
- `grass_warm_meadow_soft.png`: warm yellow-green meadow; suitable for sunny fields and dry transition zones.
- `grass_marsh_teal_soft.png`: cool teal damp grass without visible water; suitable for marsh and rain biomes.
- `grass_sparse_earth_soft.png`: sage grass with large irregular exposed-earth islands; useful as a natural grass/dirt transition.

## Dirt

- `dirt_red_clay_soft.png`: muted terracotta clay; suitable for warm zones and worn arena ground.
- `dirt_dark_humus_soft.png`: cool dark forest soil with sparse moss; suitable under trees and around ruins.
- `dirt_sandy_loam_soft.png`: pale tan sandy loam; suitable for riverbanks and dry open terrain.
- `dirt_mineral_ochre_soft.png`: ochre mineral earth with sparse cool stone chips; suitable near mines, cliffs, and mineral streams.

## Output

- `raw/`: original 1024x1024 GPT Image paintings.
- `final/`: Fourier periodic-corrected and wrap-aware upscaled 2048x2048 PNG textures.
- `previews/`: 2x2 tiling checks and contact sheet.
- `preview.html`: adjustable live repetition preview.

For best results, use these as low-frequency base surfaces and scatter separate grass, pebble, flower, and rock decals with randomized position, rotation, scale, and opacity.
